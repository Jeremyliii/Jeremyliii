#### 🎬 [journal-video-maker](https://github.com/Jeremyliii/journal-video-maker)

An unattended pipeline that turns newly published journal papers into short explainer videos, in Chinese and English.

- Picks a fresh paper, writes the script, fact-checks the numbers, and has Claude and GPT review each other's drafts
- Checks the voice-over for misread characters and awkward line breaks before anything goes out
- Renders with HyperFrames / Remotion, plus block-figure 3D scenes (three.js) designed per video
- Queues finished videos for Douyin and TikTok and watches their review status

`Python` `three.js` `Playwright` `Claude` `GPT`

#### 📈 [us-stock-insight](https://github.com/Jeremyliii/us-stock-insight)

A personal US stock research system: daily data after the close, rule-based buy lines and sell signals, a Nasdaq take-profit gauge, and twice-weekly supply-chain reports written by Claude. Pushes a short phone notification when something changes.

`Python` `Node.js` `React` `yfinance` `Claude`
